How it works

Four plain steps, every deal.

No mystery, no maze. Every lot that crosses this desk follows the same path from the grower's barn to your dock — and I handle each step myself.

1 · Intake & license check

A licensed grower puts a lot on the desk with a current lab report. I check the license against the state registry and read the report on total THC, THCA counted.

2 · A call to the lab

I call the lab that issued the report. They pull it by its number and read the numbers back to me. If anything doesn't match the PDF, the lot isn't shown.

3 · A short agreement on each side

You and the seller each sign a short agreement that states my fee: 5% from each side, only when a deal closes, both halves written in both agreements. Nobody sees the other's name before both agreements are signed.

4 · Introduction & delivery

I introduce you. The seller's price is in writing; the two of you agree terms directly. Want to see it first? The seller sells a sample pound at the lot price and ships it to you. Payment goes to the seller — wire or certified funds before the product moves — and the lot ships from the seller's dock on your carrier.

Why a broker at all

Both sides of a hemp deal are nervous. That's the job.

The farm worries about getting paid; the buyer worries the flower won't match the paperwork. A desk in the middle that checks the paper before anyone wastes a trip is what lets strangers trade.

You pay the farm directly; Meridian's commission is invoiced separately. Your money never sits in my account.
