December 11, 2026

The federal THC test changes December 11. Every lot on this desk already passes the new number.

On December 11, 2026, the federal definition of hemp switches to total THC — the delta-9 THC a plant shows today plus the THCA that becomes delta-9 when heated. Congress moved the date back from November 12 in the September budget bill.

Flower that passes only on delta-9 may not pass on total THC. Every lot on this desk is read on total THC now, and that is the only number I show.

One thing I can't tell you: the same law also caps THC per container in finished hemp products sold to consumers. What that means for a retail line is a question for your attorney, not for a broker.
